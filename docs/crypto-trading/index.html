Crypto questions

Can I trade crypto at weekends?

Weekend trading is available on selected crypto pairs. Check the platform for symbols open outside normal market hours.

Do I need a wallet?

No. Crypto CFDs are traded in USD without custody, storage or wallet management.

Are crypto commissions different?

Yes. Crypto trades are charged 0.10% of notional value with a minimum ticket fee of $0.05, applied on open and close.

What moves crypto prices?

Global liquidity conditions, ETF flows, exchange supply, regulation headlines and sentiment cycles.

Can I short crypto?

Yes, going short works the same as going long through the CFD structure.
