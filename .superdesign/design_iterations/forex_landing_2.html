How fast can I open an account?

Most accounts are verified in under ten minutes. Submit an ID document and proof of address, then fund your account.

What does it cost to trade?

Standard accounts include the cost in the spread. Pro and Elite accounts use raw spreads plus a fixed commission per lot.

Are my funds safe?

Client money is held in segregated accounts at tier-one banks and is never used for company operations.

Can I use automated strategies?

Yes. Expert advisors, scalping and hedging are all allowed. Elite accounts also get API and FIX access.

How do withdrawals work?

Request a withdrawal at any time. Most requests are processed within one business day with no fee from us.
